when [[galactose]] is absent
2 TFs control expression of related enzymes
	**1. GAL80** (Repressor Protein)
		- Binds to activation domain of GAL4 (Quenching expression)
		- $GAL\ 80^-$ = genes expressed in absence or presence of galactose
	**2. GAL4**
		- activation domain (quenched by GAL80)
		-DNA binding domain
		- $GAL\ 4^-$ = basal expression

- **GAL enhancer** 
	- DNA binding site of GAL4 recognizes a sequence in the enhancer.
- **GAL7** 
	- No galactose = no expression
- **GAL10**
	- No galactose = no expression
- **GAL1** 
	- No galactose = no expression
	- Continually expressed at basal levels
	- Binds to GAL80... moving it from activation domain of GAL4
