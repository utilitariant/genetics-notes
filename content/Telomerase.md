[[Enzyme]] that elongates [[telomeres]], synthesizing the repetitive nucleotide sequences found at the ends of eukaryotic chromosomes.

Telomeres protect the end of the chromosome from DNA damage or from fusion with neighboring chromosomes

up regulation in cells that are constantly dividing (stem cells, neoplasm/cancer cells).

contains telomerase RNA That is complementary to the repeating sequence of the [[telomeres]] plus a few base pairs. Bases are added to the end of the Parental DNA strand, elongating it one 6 base  sequence (TTAGGG) at a time. 

yeasts w/o telomerase, telomeres lose 3 bp per generation

Variable expression based on cell type
- Somatic cells - low Telomerase expression (on average cells shorten slightly at each division event)
- Stem, Germ, Tumor/cancer Cells - High Telomerase expression (Telomere length is maintained generation to generation)
