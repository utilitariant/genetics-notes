## Repressor Proteins

- blocks RNA poly II, or basal transcription factors

- Competition repressor protein has higher affinity for DNA sequence so it binds preferentially, instead of the activator 
- Cytoplasmic Sequestration binds to activator and prevents the activator from entering nucleus
