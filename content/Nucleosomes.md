A nucleosome core particle plus one of its adjacent DNA linkers.

An individual nucleosome core particle consists of a complex of eight [[Histone]] proteins—two molecules each of histones H2A, H2B, H3, and H4—and a stretch of double-stranded [[DNA]], 147 [[Nucleotide]] pairs long, that winds around a histone octamer.
The formation of nucleosomes converts a DNA molecule into a chromatin thread that is approximately one-third the length of the initial piece of DNA, and it provides the first level of DNA packing
