___
In short ribosomes function to create proteins for uses in the cell and across the body.

A ribosome is a RNA-Protein complex composed of many subunits.
Ribosomes recruit charged tRNAs, and synthesize [[peptide bonds]] between the amino acids they carry (via [[Peptidyl Transferase]], and enzyme in the P site of large subunit.
