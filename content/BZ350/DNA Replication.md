## Prokaryotes
only one origin of replication 
## Eukaryotes
Multiple (100s-1000s) Origins of replication
Occurs during the S Phase in the [[cell cycle]]

#### [[Pre-replication protein complex]] (initiator proteins)> 
multiple origin of replication. H bonds between Nitrogenous bases broken, creating replication bubble with 2 forks.

### [[Helicase]] > 
Extend the Replication forks bi-directionally

### [[Single-strand binding proteins]] > 
keeps the bubble open and prevents other compounds from binding to 'Open' bases

### [[Primase]] >
    - Builds RNA primer with [[hydroxyl group]] at 3' end for DNA polymerase to build off of.

Leading strand > contiguous
	Builds continuously....
DNA polymerase 3
    - builds off of 3' [[Hydroxyl Group]] of RNA primer. 
    - Reads parental/template DNA 3' to 5'. 
    - Synthesizes new DNA strand 5' to 3'
    - Proofreads parental DNA 3' to 5', exonuclease mismatched base pairs
    DNA polymerase 1
    - 5' to 3' exonuclease RNA primers
    -  Reads parental DNA 3' to 5'. 
    -  replaces cut RNA primer sections in new DNA 5' to 3'
    - Proofreads parental DNA 3' to 5', exonuclease mismatched base pair

Lagging strand > fragmented
	builds in fragments (Okazaki fragments)...
DNA polymerase 3
    - builds off of 3' [[Hydroxyl Group]] of RNA primer. 
    - Reads parental DNA 3' to 5'. 
    - Synthesizes new DNA 5' to 3'
    - Proofreads parental DNA 3' to 5', exonuclease mismatched base pairs
DNA polymerase 1
    - 5' to 3' exonuclease RNA primers
    -  Reads parental DNA 3' to 5'. 
    -  replaces cut RNA primer sections in new DNA 5' to 3'
    - Proofreads parental DNA 3' to 5', exonuclease mismatched base pairs
[[Ligase]]
    - fuse gaps (Okazaki fragments) between DNA ends together. (Create [[Phosphodiester bond]]s between phosphate and 3' Carbon of adjacent deoxyribose sugar)

[[Topoisomerase]] >
    - Cuts kinks in the double helix to prevent overwinding caused by helicase unwinding the double strand

[[Telomerase]]
    - helps to prevent shrinkage of telomeres.
    - reverse transcription expresses complementary nucleotides to TTAGGG repeating sequence on telomeres.
