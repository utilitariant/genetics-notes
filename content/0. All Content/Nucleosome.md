A nucleosome consists of a core particle and a strand of double helix DNA that wraps around it. forming a bead like structure.  
The DNA double helix that wraps around the core particle is approximately 147 [[Nucleotide]] pairs long, including the linker tails.  
#### Core Particle
The Core Particle is a Histone Octamer (A protein complex, composed of eight [[Histone]] proteins). The core particle octamer consists of 2 sets of the following histone proteins: H2A, H2B, H3, and H4. and a stretch of double-stranded [[DNA]].  

All of the histone proteins also contain a high proportion of positively charged [[Lysine]] and [[Arginine]] amino acids, which helps the core particle bind tightly to the negatively charged sugar–phosphate backbone of DNA double helix.  

#### Histone tails
Each of the histone proteins making up the core particle octamer have a long unstructured N-terminal amino acid 'tail', that extends out from the core particle.  

### DNA Condensation
The nucleosome structure functions as the first level of DNA condensation.
A double strand of DNA wrapped around nucleosomes condenses the DNA to approximately one-third the length of the initial DNA molecule.
