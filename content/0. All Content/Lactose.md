Lactose is a complex sugar, composed of 2 monosaccharides, 1 [[glucose]] and 1 [[galactose]].  
It can be [[hydrolyzed]] into the 2 constituent monomers.

***lac* operon**. 
In bacteria, lactose metabolism is controlled by the [[lac Operon]], a set of genes coding for several proteins that catalyze the breakdown of lactose. the operon is controlled by a few simple but efficient regulatory mechanisms.

# Related

- **[[Allolactose]]**, a lactose isomer.   
- **[[IPTG]]** a stand in for lactose used in labs.  

## Related Enzymes:
- **[[lac Permease]]** 
A membrane protein, that transports lactose into the cell  
- **[[beta-galactosidase]]**
catalyzes the hydrolysis of lactose, splitting it into its galactose and glucose constituents.
