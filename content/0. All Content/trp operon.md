**Tryptophan presence**
- Tryptophan <u>absent</u> in cell... Operon expressed
- Tryptophan <u>Present</u> in cell... Operon repressed
- when present Tryptophan molecules bind to $trpR$ repressor protein forming the  Tryptophan-trp repressor complex.

**Tryptophan-trp repressor complex** 
- binds to the trp operon operator sequence, blocking transcription of the operon.

**Attenuation mechanism** = ($trpL$) Leader sequence 
leader sequence is between operator and first coding sequence.
trpL RNA has different regions
	- 1-2 stem loop formation allows for 3-4 stem loop formation
	- 2-3 stem loop formation prevents 3-4 stem loop formation
	- 3-4 stem loop formation acts as a transcription terminator... if formed transcription will be terminated prematurely.

Tryptophan codon = UGG, when ribosome reaches this codon the availability of Tryptophan charged tRNA determines if the Ribosome can continue on through the sequence blocking 1-2 stem loop formation and allowing for 2-3 stem loop formation.

**Riboswitches**
	Sequence in the 5' UTR (untranslated region) of the operon, 
	(RNA leader sequences that have multiple conformations)
	can regulate transcription or translation...
	- Aptamer 
		RNA Structure that effector molecule can bind to...
	- Expression platform
		Structure of RNA that determines Transcription 
		- Antiterminator allows transcription
		- Terminator terminates transcription 
	**Transcription Regulation** 
		- If effector molecule is bound it changes the shape of the loop bringing complementary bases together. In turn flipping the antiterminator loop into a terminator loop that blocks operon transcription.
		- If effector molecule is absent the antiterminator loop persists allowing transcription to proceed.
	**Translation regulation**
		In the presence of the effector molecule, RBS (Ribosome Binding Sequence, or Shine-Dalgarno sequence) is covered via base pairing loop formation, and can not be recognized... meaning that translation does not start...

**sRNA Translational Control**
- Negative Regulation
	sRNA with sequence complementary to RBS binds to RBS on mRNA, inhibiting Translation.
- Positive Regulation
	If the RBS of an mRNA molecules is bound in aptamer of expression platform, sRNA strand can come in and bind to the bases on the other side of the loop that were bound to the RBS, this opens up the RBS to be recognized by the ribosome, and allows translation.
