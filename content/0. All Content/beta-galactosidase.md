___
Coded for by the $lacZ$ gene, in the [[Lac Operon]].  

Facilitates the breakdown of [[3. Notes/3.4 Content to be sorted/lactose|lactose]] via [[Hydrolysis]].  
Produces one [[galactose]] molecule and one [[glucose]] molecule from each lactose molecule.  
Present at very low levels in cells grown without lactose.
