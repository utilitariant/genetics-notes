# Initiation

Translation initiation is signaled by the ribosomal binding site of an mRNA, composed the **Shine-Dalgarno box** closely followed by AUG initiation codon.
The small ribosomal subunit binds to the mRNAs binding site in a way that holds the AUG initiation codon in the P site. once bound an initiator tRNA ($tRNA_{i}\ ^{fMet}$) with the anti-codon CAU, recognizes the start codon and binds to it. The large subunit then associates with the smaller subunit, along with the mRNA strand and initiator tRNA bound to the initiation codon. When the large subunit associates, the initiator protein sits in the Peptidyl site. With the entire Ribosomal complex formed, elongation can begin...
# Elongation 

the codon following the AUG initiation codon is recognized, and elongation factors bind to the corresponding charged tRNA in the cell. the elongation factors bring the charged tRNA to the A (aminoacyl) site of the ribosomal complex. once the A site is occupied, the [[Peptidyl Transferase]] enzyme present at the P site can synthesize a peptide bond between the carboxyl group of the fMET amino acid occupying the P site and the amino group of the amino acid occupying the A site. The formation of the peptide bond releases the amino acid from the 3' end of the tRNA that occupies the P site. Elongation factors move the Ribosome down the mRNA one codon at a time towards the 3' end of the mRNA.
