## General Info
#### N terminus (Amino group)
$5'\ [\ce{NH3}]-$ 
- 5' terminal end of polypeptide chain w/ free [[Amino group]] 
-  (Amino group at the start of a protein)

#### C Terminus (Carboxyl Group)
$-\ce{[COOH]}\ 3'$ 
- 3' terminal end of polypeptide chain w/ free [[Carboxyl Group]] 
- (carboxyl group at the end of a protein)
#### Bonds that Form Higher Structures
- Hydrogen bonds
- Disulfide bonds (between $\ce {SH}$ groups in cystine amino acids)
___
## Primary structure 

#### Polypeptide Chain
Polymer composed of amino acid monomers
Formed by the [[Peptidyl Transferase]] enzyme in the ribosome, which catalyzes a [[Dehydration synthesis]] reaction that removes the the carboxyl and amino group from adjacent amino acids, and synthesizes a peptide bond in their place, linking the amino acids.
___
## Secondary Structures
- #### $\large \alpha$ Helix (Single Helix)
- #### $\large \beta$ Folded Sheet (Jacob's Ladder)

___
## Tertiary Structure
Complex folding of amino acids. no single 'Structure'
___
## Quaternary Structure
Two or more (tertiary structure) proteins coming together to form a larger complex
- #### Identical Subunits
	2 identical proteins that form a functional complex. the individual proteins on their own are non functional.
- #### Non-identical subunits
	2 or more different proteins that come together to form a complex.
