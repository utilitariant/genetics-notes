___
$\large{(\ce{-COCH3})}$

Consists of a [[Methyl Group]] (−CH3) single-bonded to a [[carbonyl group]] (C=O)

A component of many organic compounds, including acetic acid, the neurotransmitter acetylcholine, acetyl-CoA, acetylcysteine, acetaminophen (also known as paracetamol), and acetylsalicylic acid (also known as aspirin).

Acetyl groups are commonly transferred from [[Acetyl CoA]] to other organic molecules

Key component of [[Acetylation]], and [[Deacetylation]] reactions.
